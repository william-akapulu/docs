## About Akapulu Labs

Akapulu Labs is an AI research lab. A live call has three participants in a Daily room: the person in the browser, your bot, and the avatar.

The avatar publishes the face and the played audio. Your bot listens to the browser and produces the speech. [AkapuluAvatar](https://github.com/Akapulu/pipecat-akapulu) sends that audio to the avatar.

A [hosted link](/guides/calls/hosted-links) is a public URL. You pick an avatar and write the prompt. Anyone with the link can start the call.
