---
title: "Hosted links"
description: "A public URL that starts a live avatar call."
---

A hosted link is a public URL. The visitor opens it and clicks **Start Call**. The call uses your avatar and the prompt you wrote. It uses conversation minutes.

You do not run a bot, and the page does not hold your API key.

## What you set

Create the link in the dashboard.

- **Label.** The title on the page and in your list.
- **Avatar.** The face on the call. Use one from the [catalog](/guides/avatars/avatar-catalog).
- **System prompt.** What the conversation is for.
- **This is AI.** Whether the video shows that card. The visitor cannot turn it off.

Copy the URL from the dashboard. It ends in `/session/<token>`. Anyone with the link can start a call. The visitor stays on that page.

## Your own app

A hosted link is the shareable page. To put the avatar in your own product, create the Daily room yourself and follow [Your bot](/guides/calls/your-bot).
