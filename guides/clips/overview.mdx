A clip is a talking-avatar video you generate from a script. You send what the avatar should say (English text, or a pronunciation string), and Akapulu Labs returns spoken audio and talking video.

This is separate from [live conversations](/guides/conversations/lifecycle).

A live conversation is a real-time call: you talk to the avatar, we transcribe your speech, an LLM writes a reply, and the avatar speaks it back as a video stream.

A clip is offline. You supply the line and which avatar to use. We generate the audio and video.

<Note>
Today clips are created over the API. Creating and viewing clips in the Akapulu Labs UI is coming soon.
</Note>

## How a clip works

Create starts the job. Poll until `status` is `complete`, then download `audio_url` and `video_url`.

```
Clip  "Welcome to Acme. Thanks for watching."
  ├── phonemes returned on create
  ├── status: generating → complete
  └── audio_url + video_url (presigned HTTPS, 1 hour)
```

Use a [catalog avatar](/guides/avatars/avatar-catalog) `avatar_id`, or one of your own (private avatars require an enterprise plan).

Send **exactly one** of `text` or `phonemes`. If you send `text`, we convert it to [phonemes](/guides/clips/phonemes) and return that string on create. Pass `phonemes` yourself when you want tighter control over a word.

Optional overlays (both default `true`):

- `show_ai_label` — **This is AI**
- `show_logo` — **Made by** plus the Akapulu Labs logo (`false` needs [Starter](https://akapulu.com/pricing) or higher)

Details are in [AI content disclosure](/guides/clips/ai-content-disclosure).

## Credits

Clip jobs use the same credit pool as live conversations and LLM test sessions.

**A minute of generated talking clip = 1 credit.** Charged on create. If the job fails, that charge is refunded.


## Quickstart

**1. Create** — `POST /api/clips/create/` with `avatar_id` and `text` (or `phonemes`). Optional `name`, `show_ai_label`, `show_logo`.

You get back `id`, `status` (`generating`), `phonemes`, `estimated_seconds`, and `credits_charged`. `audio_url` and `video_url` are `null` until the files exist.

**2. Poll** — `GET /api/clips/<clip_id>/` until `status` is `complete`.

Then `audio_url` and `video_url` are hour-long download links. Call GET again if a link expired.

**3. Download** those URLs.

If `status` is `failed`, see `error`.

Full request-by-request flow: [Workflow](/guides/clips/workflow).
